Settings

Microphone. Pick the mic Bernard should listen on, then press Test and speak: the bar should move.

Voice engine. Free mode costs nothing: this device listens and speaks, and Bernard thinks on your server using Google's free Gemini allowance. ElevenLabs gives Bernard's real voice and natural back-and-forth, but uses credits. Photos always use ElevenLabs.

Work laptop (printing). Lets Bernard print and open CCP documents on your work laptop, and nothing else. On the work laptop: open this page, enter your passcode, press the button, copy the line, then paste it into Windows PowerShell and press Enter.

Who can talk to Bernard. Private means only this dashboard and app, with your passcode, can start a voice conversation with him. Public means anyone who knew his ElevenLabs agent id could.

Google accounts. The Cory Car Paints account gives Bernard the CCP inbox, drafts, calendar and Drive. Your personal account lets him read your own email (read-only, never drafts) and calendar, so he can find booking references, orders, tickets and the like when you ask.

What Bernard has learnt. He picks up lasting facts, preferences and corrections from what you say (never codes or passwords) and uses them in every conversation. Ask him "what have you learnt about me?" or say "forget that".

Telegram. Text Bernard from anywhere and get the shopping list with tick-off buttons. His alerts come here too. To link a chat: open Bernard's bot in Telegram, then send it the code below. Michelle can link her own chat the same way.

"Hey Bernard" wake word. Press the grid button left of the ask bar to put Bernard on standby. He listens using Chrome's speech recognition (on-device where your computer supports it).

Optional: for a private wake word, add a Picovoice AccessKey and your "Hey Bernud" file (Web / WASM). Both stay on this device only.
