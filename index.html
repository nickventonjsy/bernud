Settings

Microphone. Pick the mic Bernard should listen on, then press Test and speak: the bar should move.

Voice engine. Free mode costs nothing: this device listens and speaks, and Bernard thinks on your server using Google's free Gemini allowance. ElevenLabs gives Bernard's real voice and natural back-and-forth, but uses credits. Photos always use ElevenLabs.

Work laptop (printing). Lets Bernard print and open CCP documents on your work laptop, and nothing else. On the work laptop: open this page, enter your passcode, press the button, copy the line, then paste it into Windows PowerShell and press Enter.

"Hey Bernard" wake word. Press the grid button left of the ask bar to put Bernard on standby. He listens using Chrome's speech recognition (on-device where your computer supports it).

Optional: for a private wake word, add a Picovoice AccessKey and your "Hey Bernud" file (Web / WASM). Both stay on this device only.
